ZeitHawk

Craft, replay, and fuzz HTTP requests, then decode, hash, and diff what comes back — a lightweight, browser-only toolkit built in the spirit of Burp Suite's core tools: Repeater, Intruder, Decoder, and Comparer.

⚠ Authorized testing only — only point this at systems you own or are explicitly authorized to test. Every request runs from your browser, so it's bound by the same CORS rules as any web page: the target must allow cross-origin access for you to see its status, headers, or body here, and browsers never let a page override Host, Cookie, Origin, or a handful of other headers. Nothing you enter leaves your browser except the requests you explicitly send.
